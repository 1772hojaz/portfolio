My Mission

Growing up on the outskirts of Bindura, a small mining town in Zimbabwe, I witnessed firsthand the adverse effects of mining-related illnesses caused by inadequate safety equipment. This experience ignited a passion in me to address this issue and became my life's mission to solve.

My goal is to create a digital platform that enables informal sector workers to purchase affordable and reliable safety equipment through an AI-powered bulk buying system. This ambition led me towards software engineering—a field that holds the key to solving many challenges faced by my community and beyond.

My journey to becoming an aspiring software engineer is a testament to my resilience and determination to contribute positively to society. I am motivated by the challenges I have overcome and the impact I aim to make through technological solutions.
