THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Grounded in community.
Driven by possibility.

Growing up on the outskirts of Bindura, Zimbabwe, I saw how inadequate safety equipment affected people working in mining. That experience shaped my interest in software: building tools that respond to real needs.

My mission is to help informal sector workers access affordable, reliable safety equipment through an AI-powered bulk buying platform. It’s an ambition that continues to guide what I learn and build.

More about my journey

Software engineering gives me a way to work towards that mission while exploring new problems. Across web applications, mobile tools, and systems programming, I learn by turning ideas into working projects.
